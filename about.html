About Me

I’m a TESOL educator who cares about understanding students’ goals, building positive teacher-student relationships, and continually improving my practice. I'm licensed in Utah and Hawai'i as a TESOL secondary teacher. Currently, I’m pursuing an M.A. in TESOL at Brigham Young University, with graduation expected in April 2028, and I teach writing to adult English learners at the BYU English Language Center.

My teaching experience spans across different learners and contexts including young children, teenagers, and adults. I have taught young English learners in a bilingual kindergarten in Thailand; worked with high school students in Utah and Hawaiʻi; tutored international university students in academic reading and writing at BYU-Hawai'i; supported adult English learners in community classes in Canada; and taught adult learners in an intensive English program at BYU.

Know the learner

I notice each student’s strengths, needs, and reasons for learning English. Students are at the center of my teaching, and their experiences help me build positive, respectful relationships.

Plan with purpose

I connect lesson goals and activities to what students want and need to do with English. Learning experiences should be meaningful, relevant, and responsive to the people in the room.

Respond and adjust

I pay attention to student feedback and evidence of learning, then adapt my teaching to support each learner’s next steps. Reflection helps me keep improving the learning experience.
