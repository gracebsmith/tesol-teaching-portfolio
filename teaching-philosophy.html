Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy brings together theories that affect day-to-day classes and broader beliefs about teaching. I believe in creating a negotiated syllabus with students, centering lessons on course outcomes, and maintaining an affective-humanistic approach toward students. I view the classroom environment, materials, and instructional methods as complementary elements that work together to support learning.

Course and learning outcomes frame instructional decisions. I share those goals with students, invite them to vote on projects or activities that help them achieve the outcomes, and ask why they are studying English so I can better align instruction with their needs and interests.
